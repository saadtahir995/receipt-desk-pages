What Receipt Desk does

Receipt Desk helps you scan, store and organise receipts, track spending, set budgets, and export or back up your records. You do not need an account. Your receipts and documents are kept on your device unless you choose to export, back up, sync or share them.

AI receipt extraction

When you scan or import a receipt, an AI service reads the merchant, date, totals, tax and line items. AI can misread or miss details, especially on faded, folded or partly visible receipts. Please check the extracted figures before you rely on them. Receipt Desk is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide tax, accounting, legal or financial advice, and it does not replace the original receipt where a law or an authority requires it.
