Receipt Desk support

Email info@glozion.com. Include your device model, Android version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. You can find the app version under Settings → Help & privacy. Use a sample receipt with sensitive information removed if an example is needed.

Restore or recover receipts

Open Settings → Recently deleted to restore a receipt from Trash. If you have a Receipt Desk ZIP, open Settings → Restore backup or find it under Folder backup & sync. Restoring a backup adds missing receipts to the current ledger.

Sync a shared folder

Choose the same writable shared folder on each device under Settings → Folder backup & sync. Open Activity and tap Sync on each device. The shared folder carries receipts, attachments, Trash changes, budgets and report settings. Folder sync is part of Premium.

App PIN

Open Settings → Display, privacy and reports → App PIN to set, change, or remove it. A forgotten PIN requires clearing app storage. Keep copies of any backups you want to restore afterward.

Family & Friends

Open Settings → Family & Friends; no account is needed. A Premium owner can create a group and share an invitation code, which expires after 24 hours. Up to two members share the owner's Premium. A member can leave the group or remove their family data from the same screen.

Restore Premium

Make sure the device uses the Google Play account that bought Premium, then open Settings → Premium and purchases and tap Restore purchases. Premium moves to the device you restore on. If you owned a Family & Friends group on your previous device, create a new group from this device and share a new invitation code. Contact support if Restore does not find your purchase.
