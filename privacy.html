Receipt Desk privacy policy

Publisher
Glozion

Effective date
2026-11-24

Receipts and documents

Receipt Desk stores the receipts you save, associated notes and line items, and original images and PDFs in app storage on your device. You can choose documents from your photo library or files. The app uses camera access when you choose to take a receipt photo. Local text recognition and barcode decoding run on the device. The app PIN controls access to the app and is stored separately from receipt backups.

Installation identifier

The first time the app uses an online feature it creates a random credential and keeps it in the device's secure storage. The Receipt Desk service stores only a one-way protected form of it. It is not the advertising identifier and is not linked to your name, email or Google account.

Features that transfer information

  • Exports and backup copies go to the destination you choose. Folder sync copies receipts and attachments to your selected shared folder. People with access to that folder may be able to open those copies.
  • Google Drive or OneDrive backup, when configured, uses the account you connect and stores a backup in the provider's app storage. Receipt Desk uses the provider authorization to list, write, and restore those backups.
  • AI receipt extraction runs only when you choose Extract with AI. The selected pages are sent over HTTPS to the Receipt Desk service operated by Glozion on Cloudflare Workers, where Cloudflare Workers AI reads the merchant, date, totals, tax and line items and returns them to the app for you to review. The service does not store the images or the extracted fields, and request logging is off. To apply the daily limit of free AI scans, it keeps a counter for your installation that is deleted after the day ends.
  • Exchange-rate estimates request historical currency rates. The request uses currency codes and dates. Receipt amounts are used on the device to calculate estimates.
  • Premium purchases are made through Google Play and managed by RevenueCat, a subscription service that processes purchase records for Glozion. Google Play and RevenueCat receive the purchase details, such as the product, order and purchase token. The app identifies your installation to RevenueCat with a random identifier derived on the Receipt Desk service; the app does not send your name or email address. The Receipt Desk service asks RevenueCat only whether Premium is active, to remove the AI scan limit and to enable Family & Friends.
  • Fiscal authority checks, when configured and selected, download public certificates and revocation lists. The app checks your receipt and selected signing certificate on the device. The download does not include your receipt data or certificate.
  • Family & Friends works without an account. The Receipt Desk service records a protected identifier for each participating installation, the group, its members and their role. It stores a protected representation of the invitation code and expires it after 24 hours. To limit invitation guessing it keeps short-lived attempt counters, including a protected value derived from your network address; they expire after one hour and are deleted in the next daily cleanup. The family service does not receive receipts, attachments, or receipt amounts.
  • Optional diagnostics, when enabled by you, sends aggregated feature counts or crash counts, the app version, Android API level, and a report identifier used for retry handling. The report contains no receipt contents, account details, or stack traces. The receiver and network infrastructure receive the connection information needed to deliver the request. Diagnostics reports are deleted after 90 days.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings → Clear all data to remove the local ledger and queued diagnostics. Copies you exported, backed up, or synced can be deleted at their destination. In Family & Friends, use Remove my family data to remove your membership and delete a group you own. To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat for your installation, or any other record the publisher holds, contact us using the address below. Google Play keeps its own order history under your Google account.

Local data and copies you make remain at their saved locations until you delete them. Glozion keeps support correspondence to handle your request and any follow-up. You can email us to request deletion of correspondence we hold.
